Black Education

Black Educational Attainment: High School Parity, the College Gap

About 89 percent of Black adults hold a high school diploma and one in four holds a bachelor's degree or higher — the attainment ladder, the college completion gap, and the generational trend.

Historically Black Colleges and Universities: Enrollment and Degrees

More than 100 HBCUs enroll roughly 290,000 students and award about one in six Black bachelor's degrees — an outsized share from a small sector. The federal education data.

Black College Enrollment: 2.4 Million Students

About 2.4 million Black students are enrolled in American colleges — nearly two-thirds of them women. The NCES enrollment data by sector, level, and trend.

Black Student Loan Debt: Borrowing More, Owing Longer

More than four in five Black bachelor's graduates borrow — and four years out they owe about $25,000 more than White graduates. The debt data behind the attainment numbers.

Black Teachers: Seven Percent of the Faculty, Fifteen Percent of the Students

Black teachers are about 7 percent of the public school faculty teaching a student body that is 15 percent Black — and the research says the mismatch matters. The NCES data.

Black Students in K-12: Enrollment, Schools, and Measured Outcomes

About 7.3 million Black children attend America's public schools — 15 percent of enrollment — across district, charter, and private systems. The NCES enrollment and outcomes data.

Black Graduate and Professional Degrees: The Top of the Pipeline

Black students earn roughly one in eight master's degrees and a smaller share of doctorates — the pipeline into medicine, law, and the professoriate. The NCES data.

Black Students in Community Colleges: The Open Door and Its Bottleneck

About a million Black students attend community colleges — the largest single sector of Black higher education — where the transfer bottleneck decides their bachelor's outcomes.

School Funding in Majority-Black Districts: The Revenue Data

School districts serving mostly Black students receive thousands less per pupil than mostly White districts in the revenue data — a gap built by the funding formulas themselves.

Black Principals and School Leaders: The Administration Pipeline

About one in ten public school principals is Black — and about one in twenty superintendents — in the NCES and association workforce data. The leadership pipeline profile.

Black Students in STEM: Degrees, Fields, and the HBCU Pipeline

Black students earn about 9 percent of STEM bachelor's degrees — with HBCUs producing a quarter of them from 3 percent of enrollment. The NSF data profile.

School Discipline and Black Students: The Federal Civil Rights Data

Black students are 15 percent of public school enrollment and receive roughly a third of suspensions in the federal Civil Rights Data Collection. The discipline profile.

Black Homeschooling and School Choice: The Fastest Shift in the Enrollment Data

Black homeschooling rates recorded the largest jump of any group in the pandemic-era surveys — from about 3 percent to the mid-teens — and have stayed elevated. The enrollment profile.

Black Students in Gifted Programs and Advanced Courses: The Identification Gap

Black students are about 15 percent of public school enrollment but under 10 percent of gifted programs — the under-identification gap in the federal data.

Black Students in Special Education: The Over-Identification Data

Black students are identified for special education at rates above their enrollment share — concentrated in the judgment-based categories. The IDEA data.

Black Law Students and Legal Education: The Pipeline to a Thin Profession

Black students are about eight percent of law school enrollment — feeding a profession where Black lawyers hold about five percent. The ABA and LSAC data.

Black Adult Learners and Workforce Credentials: The Other Half of Higher Education

Millions of Black adults hold certificates and licenses rather than degrees — the workforce credential economy the attainment tables only partly see. The NCES and labor data.

Black Superintendents and School Boards: Leading Systems They Rarely Head

Black educators lead a small share of America's school districts — concentrated in the majority-Black systems of the South and the big cities. The leadership data.

Black Students in Catholic and Private Schools: The Parallel Tradition

About one in twelve Black students attends a private school — the Catholic schools of the old cities above all, with a modern independent and charter-adjacent layer. The NCES data.

Black Children in Early Childhood: Head Start, Pre-K, and the First Gap

Black children enroll in preschool at high rates — and the system's quality and discipline data already diverge before kindergarten. The early childhood file.

Black Students and Reading: The Literacy Data From NAEP to the Science of Reading

Reading is where the achievement data begin — fourth-grade NAEP scores, the science-of-reading reforms, and the home literacy environment. The literacy file.

Black Students at Elite Colleges: The Post-2023 Enrollment Data

After the Supreme Court ended race-conscious admissions, Black enrollment in the newest classes at several elite colleges fell sharply. The institutional data.

Advanced Placement and Computer Science: Black Students' Access Data

Black students take AP courses at rising rates but remain underrepresented in calculus, physics, and computer science — often because their schools don't offer them. The College Board and Code.org data.

School Counselors and the Advising Gap: Who Gets a Guide

American schools average over 400 students per counselor; Black students' access to college advising runs through the thinnest-staffed offices. The ASCA and federal data.
