The leading resource on Black American Population Data and Insights.

Black America Data is a demographic reference covering the Black population of the United States.

Every article is built from published federal and institutional data, with the source named on the page.

The reference is organized in six sections: census, business, public health, education, employment, and cities.

How it is built

Each article presents figures drawn from published federal and institutional sources — including the U.S. Census Bureau and the American Community Survey, the Bureau of Labor Statistics, the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, and the National Center for Education Statistics — with the source named on the page and a data chart summarizing the headline figures.

Pages in this reference carry no publication dates; the figures on each page are the published figures from the named source, presented so the reference stays usable as a standing data resource.
